Privacy Policy

At My E-commerce, your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email, address, phone number.
  • Payment Details: Collected securely during checkout (not stored on our servers).
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, and device/browser details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your orders and deliver products.
  • To send updates, promotions, or important service notices.
  • To improve our website and user experience.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We may share limited data with:

  • Trusted delivery partners to complete orders.
  • Payment processors to facilitate secure transactions.
  • Legal authorities if required by law.

4. Data Security

We use encryption, firewalls, and secure servers to protect your information from unauthorized access.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your data.
  • Request corrections or deletions.
  • Opt out of marketing at any time.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to personalize your experience and analyze traffic. You can adjust your cookie settings in your browser.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a revised date.

If you have questions, please contact us.

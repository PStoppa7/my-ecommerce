Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You’ll be guided step-by-step through the process.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and EFT (bank transfer). All transactions are secured and encrypted.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days depending on your location.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes, we offer a 7-day return/exchange policy on eligible items. Please check our return policy page for full details.

How do I track my order?

After placing your order, you’ll receive a tracking number via email. Use it to follow your shipment online in real time.

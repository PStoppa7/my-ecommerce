Shipping Information

We deliver your orders quickly and reliably. Here's what you need to know:

Delivery Options

  • Standard Shipping: 3–5 business days (free on orders over R500)
  • Express Shipping: 1–2 business days (R99 flat rate)
  • Same-Day Delivery: Available in select areas (R149)

Shipping Locations

  • Delivery available throughout South Africa.
  • International shipping on request — contact us first.

Order Processing

  • Orders before 2PM are processed same day.
  • After 2PM, processing begins the next business day.

Tracking Orders

  • Tracking sent via SMS or email after dispatch.
  • Track anytime from your ShopEase account.
